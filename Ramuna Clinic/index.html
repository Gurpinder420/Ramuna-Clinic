Our Services

Comprehensive Diabetes Care

From diagnosis to long-term management, we provide complete care across all aspects of diabetes and metabolic health.

Diabetes Screening & Diagnosis
Comprehensive blood glucose testing, HbA1c analysis, and early detection of pre-diabetes conditions with advanced diagnostic tools.
Personalized Diabetes Management
Customized treatment plans including medication management, insulin therapy, and continuous glucose monitoring for optimal control.
Diet & Lifestyle Counseling

Diabetic Foot Care

Conditions We Treat

Managing Diabetes Complications

Uncontrolled diabetes can affect multiple organ systems. Dr. Singh provides expert care for all diabetes-related complications.

Alzheimer's Risk
Diabetes significantly increases dementia risk. Early metabolic control reduces cognitive decline and neurological complications.
Cardiovascular Disease
Managing blood glucose, cholesterol, and blood pressure to significantly reduce heart attack and cardiac event risks.
Depression & Anxiety
Addressing the psychological burden of diabetes through integrated mental health support and holistic treatment approaches.
Foot Damage & Ulcers
Prevention and treatment of diabetic foot wounds, ulcers, and neuropathic complications to avoid serious outcomes.
Kidney Disease (Nephropathy)
Early detection and management of diabetic kidney damage to protect renal function and prevent chronic kidney disease.
Neuropathy
Treatment of diabetes-induced nerve damage causing pain, numbness, and tingling in extremities with proven therapies.
Eye Disease (Retinopathy)
Monitoring and management of diabetes-related eye complications to preserve vision and prevent blindness.
Stroke Risk
Comprehensive vascular risk reduction through optimal diabetes management, blood pressure control, and lifestyle intervention.
